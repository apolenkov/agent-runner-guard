# Getting help

- **Questions, bugs and ideas**: open an
  [issue](https://github.com/apolenkov/agent-runner-guard/issues/new/choose). Include your
  Node version (`node --version`), the command the guard wrapped, and the last
  line of its output (the verdict).
- **Security problems**: do not open a public issue; follow
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) to report privately.
- **Working on the code**: see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
